# Chronicle trademark policy

The Chronicle name and project logo identify the official Chronicle project at
<https://github.com/SimpleOpenSoftware/chronicle>. This policy helps people
distinguish the official project from independently maintained forks and services.

## Software freedom

You may use, modify, redistribute, sell, and host Chronicle under the applicable
software license. Commercial use and competing products are welcome, subject to
that license. This policy addresses branding and does not change the permissions
or source-sharing obligations in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The software license does not grant rights under trademark law to use the
Chronicle name or logo as your own product or service branding. This reservation
is consistent with AGPLv3 section 7(e).

## Forks and independent services

Use your own name and logo for a publicly offered fork, modified distribution,
or independently operated service. Do not use Chronicle's name, logo, or
confusingly similar branding in a way that suggests your offering is the official
Chronicle project or is affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by it without
prior written permission from the relevant trademark holder.

This does not require rebranding a private installation or removing copyright
notices, license notices, or accurate attribution from source code.

## Truthful references

You may refer to Chronicle truthfully in documentation, comparisons, reviews,
compatibility statements, and attribution. For example:

> ExampleApp is based on Chronicle and is independently maintained. It is not
> affiliated with or endorsed by the Chronicle project.

You may use screenshots to discuss or document Chronicle when the context does
not misleadingly suggest endorsement. Nothing in this policy limits fair use,
nominative use, or other rights available under applicable law.

## Permission

For branding uses beyond this policy, contact the maintainers through the
[official repository](https://github.com/SimpleOpenSoftware/chronicle) to request
permission from the relevant trademark holder. Do not assume permission from
silence. This policy does not claim that any mark is registered.
